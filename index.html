Before you shoot

  • Wash and vacuum it, then take out every personal item, phone mount and air freshener.
  • Brush off all snow and slush. Buyers assume it is hiding something.
  • Shoot in open shade or the hour before sunset. Noon sun means glare and hard shadows.
  • Pick a plain background: an empty lot or a blank wall, with no other cars in frame.
  • Hold the phone sideways and leave a little space around the whole car.
  • Lead the listing with your cover photo, then post the rest in this order.

    Buyers

    Before a test drive

    • Meet in daylight in a busy public place, or at home with someone else there.
    • Look at their driver's licence and take a photo of it.
    • Check that insurance covers them as the driver, yours or theirs.
    • Ride along, and keep the keys until you are both in the car.
    • Count cash together; confirm a bank draft with the bank that issued it.
    • Don't sign over ownership until the payment has fully cleared.
